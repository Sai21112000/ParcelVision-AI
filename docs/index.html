How it works

1

Frame the receipt

The live camera looks for document edges anywhere in view. A yellow guide helps you line it up.

2

Hold still

When the receipt is stable for 10 frames, a short countdown auto-captures. No shutter button.

3

Confirm the scan

We warp, binarize, and reject blur. Then one tap uploads — or retake and try again.
